At Stevens Institute of Technology, I investigated EEG-based models for mental health disorder diagnosis with an emphasis on interpretability and diagnostic reliability.

The work compared convolutional, recurrent, and graph neural network approaches for capturing spatial-temporal patterns and brain connectivity structures, and also included a literature review on modern neural architectures for EEG diagnosis and prognosis.
