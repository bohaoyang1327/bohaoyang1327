Advised by Dr. Bibhas Chakraborty at Duke-NUS Medical School, I lead this project, which predicts how long orthodontic treatment will take by combining intraoral photographs with clinical records.

The work builds image–clinical fusion models, including vision encoders and small vision-language models, and pairs them with survival models that properly handle patients who are still in treatment (right-censored outcomes).
