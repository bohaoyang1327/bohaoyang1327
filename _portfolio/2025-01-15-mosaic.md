`MOSAIC` is a multi-agent framework for multi-framework communication coding in clinician-patient dialogue. The system combines LangGraph-based orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, domain-adaptive retrieval, and dynamic few-shot prompting.

I helped design and evaluate the framework on 70 clinical transcripts, improving human alignment to above 93% and F1 to above 90%. I also worked on multimodal text-audio pair generation, large language model comparisons, and manuscript preparation covering methods, ablations, fairness, and robustness.
