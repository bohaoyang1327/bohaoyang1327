`MOSAIC` (Multi-framework Structured Agentic AI system for Clinical Communication) is a LangGraph-based system for scalable and reliable coding of clinician-patient conversations across communication frameworks and clinical domains. The work has been accepted for publication in *npj Digital Medicine*.

The architecture coordinates four components: a **Plan Agent** that selects codebooks and organizes the workflow; an **Update Agent** that maintains retrieval databases; specialized **Annotation Agents** that combine codebook-guided retrieval-augmented generation with dynamic few-shot prompting; and a **Verification Agent** that checks consistency, resolves conflicts, and feeds representative examples back into the system.

MOSAIC was developed using 26 gold-standard annotated transcripts and evaluated on 50 test transcripts from rheumatology and OB/GYN encounters. It achieved an overall F1 score of **0.928**, including **0.962** on the rheumatology subset, with particularly strong performance for patient-behavior coding. Ablation studies further showed that the full agentic system outperformed baseline approaches.
