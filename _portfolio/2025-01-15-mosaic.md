`MOSAIC` (Multi-framework Structured Agentic AI system for Clinical Communication) is a LangGraph-based system for scalable and reliable coding of clinician-patient conversations across communication frameworks and clinical domains. The work was published in *npj Digital Medicine* in October 2026.

The architecture coordinates four components: a **Plan Agent** that selects codebooks and organizes the workflow; an **Update Agent** that maintains retrieval databases; specialized **Annotation Agents** that combine codebook-guided retrieval-augmented generation with dynamic few-shot prompting; and a **Verification Agent** that checks consistency, resolves conflicts, and feeds representative examples back into the system.

MOSAIC was developed on 54 gold-standard annotated transcripts and evaluated on 120 transcripts spanning rheumatology and OB/GYN encounters. It achieved an overall weighted F1 score of **95.6%**, with the highest performance in rheumatology (**95.9%**) and the strongest results for the bias and patient-behavior categories (**96.4%** and **96.1%**, respectively).
