Developed at Duke University from January 2025 to May 2026, `MOSAIC` is a multi-agent framework for multi-framework communication coding in clinician-patient dialogue. Accepted for publication in *npj Digital Medicine*, the system combines LangGraph-based orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, domain-adaptive retrieval, and dynamic few-shot prompting.

I designed and evaluated the framework on 70 clinical transcripts (approximately 20 hours of audio), improving human alignment to above 93% and F1 to above 90%. I also worked on aligned multimodal text-audio pair generation, audio feature analysis, large language model comparisons, and manuscript preparation covering methods, ablations, fairness, and robustness.
