This project extends [`MOSAIC`]({{ '/research/mosaic/' | relative_url }}) toward clinical communication coding that stays accurate while costing less and running faster. I am a co-first author, advised by Dr. Chuan Hong at Duke University.

We are building a multilingual synthetic benchmark of clinician-patient dialogues annotated across three established communication codebooks. On top of it, we are developing lightweight decision models and a confidence-based agentic workflow that handles easy cases cheaply and sends uncertain ones to stronger large language models.
