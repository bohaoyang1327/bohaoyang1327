This project focused on building an end-to-end ambient digital scribing system for clinical settings. The pipeline integrated voice activity detection, Whisper-based speech recognition, and speaker diarization powered by voice embeddings.

The system was used to generate structured medical notes for clinicians and paired with human-in-the-loop evaluation to improve factual consistency, transcription quality, and practical usability in routine documentation workflows.
