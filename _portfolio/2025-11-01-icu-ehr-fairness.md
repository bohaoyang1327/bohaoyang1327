This ongoing project studies racial and ethnic bias in ICU mortality prediction with the goal of developing equitable and clinically deployable prognostic models.

I have worked on preprocessing EHR data from Duke University and the Medical University of South Carolina, cohort construction, and the evaluation of baseline mortality models such as SOFA, APACHE II, and time-to-event approaches including Cox models.
