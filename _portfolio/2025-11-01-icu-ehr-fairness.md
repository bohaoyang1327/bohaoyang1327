From November 2025 to April 2026, this project studied racial and ethnic bias in ICU mortality prediction with the long-term goal of developing equitable and clinically deployable prognostic models.

I preprocessed large-scale EHR data from Duke University and the Medical University of South Carolina, assisted with cohort construction, and evaluated mortality models such as SOFA, APACHE II, and time-to-event approaches including Cox proportional hazards models, with a focus on robustness and fairness.
