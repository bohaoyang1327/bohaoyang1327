`SAGE` is a structured agent for identifying clinical cohorts from electronic health records using ICD-9/10 diagnosis codes and longitudinal event tracking. The project emphasizes reusable phenotyping logic and scalable extraction pipelines across multi-source health data.

I contributed to the SQL-based agent design, assisted with diagnosis-date identification and longitudinal incidence tracking, supported testing and documentation, and mentored junior students working on inflammatory bowel disease phenotyping and related cohort extraction workflows.
