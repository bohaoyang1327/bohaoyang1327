At the Imperial College London Winter School, I developed a machine learning workflow for credit default prediction using real-world credit data. The project included data cleaning, exploratory visualization, model comparison, and deployment.

The best-performing XGBoost model achieved strong predictive performance and was packaged with FastAPI and Docker for scalable risk scoring.
