Advised by Dr. Bibhas Chakraborty at Duke-NUS Medical School, I develop reinforcement learning algorithms and trial designs for mobile health interventions.

* **COBRA**: a wearable-based lifestyle intervention.
* **FamNucleus**: a postpartum mental-health app.
* **EMPOWER**: a just-in-time adaptive intervention for physical activity in diabetes care, where I play a supporting role.

Across these studies, I design micro-randomized trials and adaptive personalization methods that decide when and how to send nudges to participants.
