Published in *npj Digital Medicine* (October 2026), this article presents `MOSAIC`, a multi-agent framework for analyzing clinician-patient communication across multiple coding frameworks. The project combines retrieval-augmented generation, adaptive prompting, multimodal alignment, and human-in-the-loop evaluation to improve both coding accuracy and clinical usability.
